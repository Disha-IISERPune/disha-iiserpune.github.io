[](/uploads/2013/01/someshwarwadilaman-basti-science-camp-3.jpg)Why a new centre? The success of Someshwarwadi centre was the motivation for the centre at Laman Vasti. Laman Vasti abuts Someshwarwadi. It is mainly inhabited by Lamans, which was a nomadic tribe. Kids from Laman Vasti do not come to Someshwarwadi due to social rifts. Hence we decided to open a new centre for them.

Started initially with a temple building as the classroom base, this centre has grown in leaps and bounds in a year. Recognising difficulties in continuing with the location as the classroom, other options were explored. Finally after collaborating with an NGO which had been working in the locality, Disha took over the teaching and the space from them. It is currently a smooth running programme with many students and sufficient volunteer base.

In the long-term, Disha aims at building a good library for the community and also interacting with the parents more so that they play a vital role in shaping their child's future.
