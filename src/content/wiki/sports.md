Apart from Football and Cricket ( by the way,these also were thoroughly enjoyed) many other innovative games were played

- **_Rumal Pani_** :in which same numbered players from opposite teams competed for the property of a handkerchief placed in the middle. You are out if you couldn’t get    he handkerchief back or if you are tapped by the opponent while in possession of it.
-  **_Kombda Yuddh_** :in which, again, same numbered players from opposing teams competed to catch the other off balance while hopping holding the other leg with one hand. The team with maximum individual points wins.
- _**Ghodesavar Yuddh**:._ In this game, each team has to have even number of participants and they have to carry one on the back. When the time starts each pair has to enter the field and start pushing and shoving until the opponent loses balance. The team with the last pair standing wins.
-  **_Jahaaz_** _**Yuddh :**_ in which two circles have to be mad by locking hands of team members. Each team has to try and break the other circle. 
- **_Dharmaguru_** was another game played in which there is a designated _dharmaguru_ for each team who sits at a location. The members of one team have to catch hold of the members of the other team and bring them to the _dharmaguru_. They are out of the game if the opponent _dharmaguru_ touches him. The game ends if all members of one team including the _dharmaguru_ are out of the game.
- _**Kho-kho**_
- _**Chirghodi** (_in which a team makes a long line bending and holding the legs of the one in front and the other team members have to leap over them and test the strength of this ‘bridge’).
