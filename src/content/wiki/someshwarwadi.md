Sanjay Gandhi Vasahat is a small vasti on the road to Someshwarwadi. It is an amalgamated society of people with different social and economic states. The abhyasika at the Samaj mandir (a common meeting place) in this vasti is the first initiative by Disha. This is the third year of this activity. Initially, 10th class students were targeted. All of them secured good marks in their exams and importantly they all are willing to help us in teaching young kids. After expansion and much experimentation, we teach children throughout the week. We also conduct some art sessions especially for girls in the weekends. In the past two years, many events have been organised in this locality such as a celebration during the Ganesh festival, a Science camp, a Diwali food stall at IISER selling sweets made by the women and even a _diya_ stall.
