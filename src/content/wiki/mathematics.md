- **MATHEMATICS**

**Coming up with conjectures through the use of Straws and Thread :**

Students were divided into groups.Each group was provided with straws of various lengths.The students made simple shapes like triangles with various combination of straws. They were asked to examine the conditions when a triangle could be formed and to try and come up with explanations.After discussions within groups and among different groups the students successfully came up with the **Triangle Inequality.**

[**Konisberg Bridge Problem**](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Bridges_of_K%C3%B6nigsberg) :

A small model of the problem was made in the school ground. The students walked around the model.The kids came up with different reasons for the problem and a few were really close. Anisa ma’am then explained in very simple words the solution .

**Euler’s characteristics for planner graph (V – E + F = 2):**

The idea of the bridge problem was extended to various planar graphs. The kids were encouraged to solve these problems in an interactive manner.

**Modular arithmetic** :

Simple modular arithmetic and base system was taught to the students.

**Prisoner Dilemma using card game :**

Simple card games were played. **Mobius band :** 

The geometry of a cylinder and a Mobius band were compared by first making such objects by paper and their properties were studied by cutting and analyzing the objects made.
