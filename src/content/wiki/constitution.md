> **Disclaimer:** Please note that the following constitution and mission statements are from 2013. Things have changed over time and the goals are no longer the same.

The complete constitution of Disha can be found [here](/uploads/2013/04/disha_constitution.pdf).

**I.** **Vision:**

1. Creating a sustainable environment which will provide equal opportunities for all towards holistic development
2. Creating a culture of “thinking beyond self” among the youth

**II.** **Mission:**

1. Removing education inequity by providing necessary forms of education to the underserved children through voluntary work
2. Inspiring children to become self-dependent
3. Creating a force of youth who are proactive towards bringing about social change

**III.** **Goals:**

\[Time bound for one year\]

1. Developing database of activities for Disha
2. Developing relationship with parents of students at abhyasikas
3. Organizing one training workshop for volunteers/members per semester
4. Conducting annual Disha day with focus on the following

- Exhibition of activities (in the form of posters)
- Fund raising
- Meeting with advisory committee
- General meeting
