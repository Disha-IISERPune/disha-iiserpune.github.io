The imagination and creativity of children (as well as the volunteers) approached new heights in the craft sessions.

The various works included

- **Origami** :

Beautiful birds, shapes and flowers were made by folding paper.

- **Bandhani :**

Tying and dyeing

- **Leaf Printing :**

The children painted design onto paper using leaves dipped in paint.

 

- **Embroidery** :

Students made handkerchiefs with mended sides and the corner decorated with flowers made of French knot and single chain stitches

- **Making newspaper caps:**

Different types of caps were mad efrom old newspapers. The activity was thoroughly enjoyed.
