-  **BIOLOGY EXPERIMENT**

1. Onion peel cells
2. leaf cells
3.  flower petal cells
4. pollen grains
5.  stem
6.  _Spirogyra_ (in water) etc.
7. Osmosis

 They were taught how to make temporary mounts and how to focus things under a microscope. The volunteers also taught them how to dissect a flower and observe its parts and this was done using a _Hibiscus_.
