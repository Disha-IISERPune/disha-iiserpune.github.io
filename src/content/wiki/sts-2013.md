Disha volunteers visited villages around Pune on the 4 weekends of February 2013 as a part of ‘Spread The Smile!’ program. It was a life changing experience for all the volunteers.

Spread the Smile was conducted in six villages around Pune. The villages visited by the volunteers were Vetale and Kadadhe near Rajgurunagar; Ambavane, Hatve and Wazeghar near Velhe and Kurunji near Bhor.

Spread the Smile! is back in 2013 with its second edition. It is bigger and better this year with it expanding to six villages around Pune.
