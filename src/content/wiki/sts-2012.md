Disha volunteers visited villages around Pune on the 4 weekends of March 2012 as a part of 'Spread The Smile!' program. It was a life changing experience for all the volunteers.

The villages visited by the volunteers were Mangdari-Katawadi-Kolawadi, Kusgaon, Asde and Salumbre.

For details about activities conducted at each place,

\* [Mangdari and Katawadi-Kolawadi](http://wp.me/P2ehIt-1r)

\* [Kusgaon](http://wp.me/P2ehIt-1n)

\* [Asde](http://wp.me/P2ehIt-1x)

\* [Salumbre](http://wp.me/P2ehIt-1B)

The categorical distribution of activities carried out can be found in the [archives](/wiki/archives) section.
