Until Fall 2012, the activities of Disha were limited to Pune, in terms of location, and IISER, in terms of volunteer base. Recognising an immediate need for expansion of this burgeoning programme, the Outreach Team was constituted.

The Outreach team aims to base itself on the fundamental principles of Disha but spread its reach large and wide. It began with the '[Spread the Smile](/programs/spread-the-smile)' programme at four villages around Pune in March 2012. The Outreach Team decide to take this initiative further by continuing activities at one of the villages, Kusgaon. The team sets long-term goals of rural development through small projects and initiatives in format easily undertaken by children. The aim is to inspire the children to feel the need for improvement in amenities and conditions of their home and village and to induce them to take initiative.
