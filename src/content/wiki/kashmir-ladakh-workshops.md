**Kashmir - Ladakh Tour : Science Workshops for school students**

Science outreach activities were conducted by a group of volunteers from Pune, closely associated with Disha, a voluntary organization of students of Indian Institute of Science Education & Research (IISER) – Pune and Jnana Prabodhini, Pune. The group of six volunteers traveled across Kashmir-Leh Ladakh area from 7th May to 22nd May, 2013 to conduct science workshops for students in 12 schools in remote areas. The organizations that supported us to get connected with the schools were, Aseem Foundation and Nature Conservation Foundation (NCF). The workshops that would typically run for 3-4 hours in a school for 5th to 8th standard students were designed, to convey joy and excitement in science through science demonstrations, discussions, hands-on experiments; and to encourage students to develop interest in scientific thinking.
