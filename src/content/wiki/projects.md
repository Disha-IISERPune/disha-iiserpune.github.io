Projects were initiated amongst the children wherein we tried to address the various problems in the villages and come up with solutions. Information Charts , 3 dimensional models using cardboard and soil and Role plays were a part of this endeavour.

The projects were

**Constructing model of India’s National River linking Project(nrlp) using map and LEDs.**

Students were given plan of nrlp now they have to point all rivers in plan in large map and draw the path of rivers and their connecting links. They’ve attached each link with a LED. There are two parts in nrlp plan, we connected LEDs in series such a way that we can on the lights of one part of plan at a time so that we can differentiate two parts clearly.
