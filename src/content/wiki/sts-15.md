**What is Spread the Smile?**

Spread the Smile(STS) program is an initiative of Disha wherein volunteers visit different villages near Pune during weekends to perform education related activities for high school students.

**What are the objectives of the program?** 

1\. To spread the **joy** of science among village students.

2\. To **expose** students to different themes of science, technology and

3\. To **inspire** students for higher studies and inspire volunteers to be proactive change makers in our society.

**When is the program?**

 

Spread the Smile '15 is designed for the following weekends during January-February:

17th - 18th Jan

24th - 25th Jan

31st Jan - 1st Feb

7th - 8th Feb

You can participate in any (and more than one) weekend of your choice.

**Who can volunteer for the program?**

Anyone! Anyone interested in guiding and nurturing students.

College students are highly encouraged to join and volunteer for Spread the Smile.

**What specific activities will be conducted?**

- Science Experiments and Demonstrations
- Arts and Craft Sessions
- Field Activities like Map Making, Nature Walk,etc
- Sport sessions
- Mentoring Student Projects
- Conducting discussions

**How do I design the different activities that are to be conducted?**

All the volunteers assigned to a village for a particular weekend will design the activities. The program coordinators and village coordinators will lead and guide in this process.

All the materials will be provided by Disha.
