The IISER campus, today, is home to hundreds of construction workers. Their kids are not getting education in any form. The nature of their parents' jobs makes the family move very often, thus not allowing them to go to school either. So it seemed necessary to address this problem. With this motivation, we opened our newest center in IISER campus itself.

The classes were begun humbly under streetlights and then with a lot of help from the IISER administration as well as the construction company, a shed was allocated for Disha activities. With ample room, a blackboard and electric lighting, this room provides well for the unfortunate children. These children like getting involved in the celebrations at IISER and they are welcomed as part of the IISER family.
