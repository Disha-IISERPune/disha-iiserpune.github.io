---
title: "Spread the Smile!"
date: "2012-04-12"
---

Disha volunteers visited villages around Pune as a part of 'Spread The Smile!' program.It was a life changing experience for all the volunteers.

  The villages visited by the volunteers were Mangdari, Katawadi-Kolawadi, Kusgaon, Asde and Salumbre.
