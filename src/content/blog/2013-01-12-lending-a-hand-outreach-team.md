---
title: "Lending a Hand - Outreach Team"
date: "2013-01-12"
---

After the success of the Spread the Smile programme, the Outreach Team was formed mainly to continue activities in one of the villages, Kusgaon. Long-term activities for government-run schools were planned under _Karyanubhav_.

Under the Outreach Team's initiative, many camps and workshops have been conducted in the village of Kusgaon. A session on papier mache was conducted and during the Ganesh festival, an idol making competition was also held. Another unique and thoughtful initiative was the camp conducted by the members of Vijnan Ashram, Pabal on basic electric circuitry.

Further, long projects that deal with the broad topic of the development of their village were selected and groups were made. The children in these groups were asked to collect information from the concerned people in the village regarding their assigned topics. The activities of the project will go on over the semester. The whole activity will teach them at least one new skill, the subtleties in interacting with people and basic skills of problem solving.

The Outreach Team also plans to make a proper module of the sessions and get them implemented in many rural government schools.
