It has been quite a while since any blog was posted on the web page, but the activities of Disha have still been ongoing and never really stopped. Since it has been this long, perhaps it is a good idea to mention a few things. It is safe to assume that activities in Disha are ongoing even if this page does not get updated (the contacts on the webpage are usually up to date); the best way to reach out to us if you are from outside is through email at (disha@sac.iiserpune.ac.in). Now for the journey of Disha. We had been functioning regularly during the times of 2018-20, evolving details of our program continuously as and when needed. Our workings shifted to online mode when the pandemic hit. Abhyasika, Mindspark, Prerna, and SNP all took classes online. A lot of challenges and leanings through the experience. Programs experimented with online demonstrations and interactive simulations, successfully staying afloat. We also held a donation drive to fund the doorstep school to take care of the school for on-campus kids of construction workers during the pandemic. The year 2021-22 has been a hybrid state. We started with online classes, and by the end of the term, we shifted to offline classes for almost all the programs. STS is also in plans as the blog is being written. Additionally, the open events restarted with Disha day celebrations, including dinner. For the first time, we released Disha merchandise. A lot of plans were fulfilled, and a lot of them are ready for a beta rollout (We will perhaps put a separate post detailing them). We hope the beta plans take shape and even fresher ideas get planned as a new batch of people now take up the responsibility to take Disha forward.
