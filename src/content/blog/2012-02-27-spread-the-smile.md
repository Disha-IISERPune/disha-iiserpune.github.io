Disha is visiting villages around Pune for four weekends starting from **10-11th March.**

The purpose is to spread the awareness of science amongst children in rural areas in a friendly and innovative manner. The focus would be on interactive fun games, experiments and demonstrations,art and craft, role-plays and sports.
