---
title: "Outreach Team"
date: "2012-09-20"
---

The Outreach team is a new limb growing out of Disha which handles the activities of Disha besides its permanent centre Abhyasikas. This team focuses on activities beyond the regular centres, especially in the villages around Pune. The target population is school children in rural schools and the aim is to provide them with exposure to science and other fun activities and to help them contribute to the development of the village scene.

From August, every weekend, Disha volunteers have been visiting Kusgaon, one of the villages that was targeted during Spread the Smile. They spend a whole day with the students, teaching them useful handicrafts like papier mache, gardening with herbal or vegetable plants and clay pottery making. The students have been very enthusiastic about these activity sessions and the team has decided to cater to specific needs of the children.

They aim to set up a vegetable or herbal garden in the school, which will be managed by the students and also make them aware of the ways in which their village can be developed.
