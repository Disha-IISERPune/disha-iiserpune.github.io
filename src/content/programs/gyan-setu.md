**Gyan-Setu**

It is a volunteering program started by Educational Activity Research Center (EARC) – Jnana Prabodhini – Pune. This volunteering program works in remote areas of developmentally challenged states of India  such as Arunachal Pradesha, Assam, Jharkhand, Chhatisgarh, Jammu & Kashmir, Nagaland, Orissa and Sikkim. Volunteer teams conduct **science-based workshops for school children** in all these states. Disha work as a collaborator in this program in terms of content development and volunteering support.

 

For more information visit:
