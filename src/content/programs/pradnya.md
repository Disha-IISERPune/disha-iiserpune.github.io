Pradnya is one of newest programmes of Disha, started in the year 2018. The idea of the programme is to create a platform to impart classroom-based structured education to children who were previously in Abhyasika. Our objective is to make the children confident about their communication and numerical skills, which are the basics that they need to go into the world out there. The volunteers focus more on personal interaction for overall development of the child rather than just academics.

**Time**: 6:30 to 7:30 PM, Wednesday and Friday

**Place**: LHC, IISER Pune
