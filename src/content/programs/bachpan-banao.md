This is a month long fellowship/volunteering program which will give you the opportunity to contribute constructively to one of India’s least developed regions. You will get a chance to work with the teachers of government schools in Dantewada district of Chhattisgarh to improve the quality of education.

**Why?**

Dantewada district has been fairly unknown due to its geographical inaccessibility. Majority of the population have minimal access to modern amenities. Literacy rate is among the lowest in India. The major problem for the teachers in government schools is the lack of exposure to various effective teaching practices available today.

**Who can apply?**

Students, working professionals and retired teachers who are at least 21 years old and are passionate about education

**When and how?**

“Fellows” are expected to participate for a minimum period of one month (which can be extended up to six months). “Volunteering” opportunity is also available with minimum duration of ten days.The program will start from 15th August 2012 and will continue till April 2013. To apply, visit [www.bachpanbanao.wordpress.com](http://bachpanbanao.wordpress.com/apply/). Fellowships will be awarded based on an interview.

**What fellows get?**

Fellows get local hospitality, travel allowance to reach Dantewada, a stipend to meet their local expenses, a letter of recognition and plenty of goodwill of the children of Dantewada! Volunteers get travel allowance and no stipend.

**Role of Disha**

Disha is responsible for fellow/volunteer management in Bachpan Banao.
