Welcome to Disha! ✨

We are a student-led social outreach club at IISER Pune. Since 2010, our volunteers have been dedicated to spreading the joy of learning, nurturing scientific curiosity, and building a stronger community.

Our Core Initiatives

🌱 Apeksha

Our newly revamped program tailored to empower and guide young minds. (Formerly Prerna)

📚 Abhyasika

Daily after-school study sessions and mentoring for students from local communities.

💡 Mindspark & Talk for Twenty

Interactive science sessions and weekly talks designed to spark curiosity and broaden horizons.
